# User-supplied research data

Place six prepared vehicle images in `vehicles/`, 27 component images in
`components/`, and 15 historical SA contour images in `sa_history/`.
Naming and preparation rules are in the main README.
The repository does not contain the original website photographs or research
contour images. Synthetic examples are generated by `make_demo_inputs.py`.
Actual-input hashes for located vehicle/component files are recorded separately
in `docs/located_input_manifest.json`. They do not constitute redistribution of data.
